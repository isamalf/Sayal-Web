Sayal Qatar — Invest in U.S. Stocks and ETFs in Qatari Riyals (QAR)

Sayal (sayal.qa) is a mobile investment and stock market app designed for investors in Doha, Qatar and across the GCC. With Sayal, you can explore thousands of U.S. stocks and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) listed on NASDAQ and NYSE—including Apple (AAPL), Microsoft (MSFT), NVIDIA (NVDA), Amazon (AMZN), Alphabet Google (GOOGL), and Meta (META)—with all prices, charts, and portfolio balances shown in Qatari Riyals (QAR) or U.S. Dollars (USD). Start risk-free with a 100,000 QAR practice portfolio in Practice Mode, set personal financial goals, and start trading whenever you are ready.
