Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short: Sayal is a paper-money investing app. We collect the details you give us at signup, use them to run your account and to tell you about Sayal, and never sell them. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Sayal ("we", "us") operates the Sayal mobile application and the website at sayal.qa. You can reach us at isamalfuqaha@gmail.com.

What Sayal is, and is not

Sayal is a simulated investing experience. Every account starts with a virtual balance of 100,000 QAR of paper money. Prices reflect real United States stock markets, but no real money is deposited, invested, or at risk, and no securities are bought or sold on your behalf. Sayal is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, or bank, and nothing in the app is investment advice.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide: first and last name, email address, mobile phone number, date of birth, country of residence, preferred language, and a password (stored only as a salted Argon2 hash; we cannot read it).
  • Your choices: whether you have agreed to receive marketing from us, and, if you tell us, how you heard about Sayal.
  • Activity in the app: your simulated orders, positions, watchlist and portfolio history, so the app can show them back to you.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs used for security, abuse prevention and rate limiting.

We do not collect government identification numbers, bank details, card details or precise location.

How we use it

  • To create and operate your account and keep you signed in.
  • To send essential service emails, such as password resets.
  • To understand interest in Sayal and, where you have consented, to contact you about Sayal, including future products. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time in Settings or by emailing us.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Legal basis

We process your data to perform our agreement with you (running your account), with your consent (marketing), and for our legitimate interests in operating a secure service and understanding demand for it.

Who we share it with

  • Alpaca Securities LLC / Alpaca Markets — provides the live market data and market calendar shown in the app. We send Alpaca the stock symbols being viewed; we do not send it your personal details.
  • Mailgun — delivers our transactional emails (your email address and the message content).
  • Hosting providers — our servers and database run on cloud infrastructure in the United States.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

International transfers

Our servers and service providers are located in the United States. Wherever you use Sayal from, your information is transferred to and stored in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, sessions, simulated orders, positions and history are permanently deleted from our live database immediately; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. Security logs are retained for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can view and edit your name, phone number and marketing preference in Settings. You can delete your account, and with it all of your data, from Settings → Delete account, or by following these instructions. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, port or restrict processing of your data, or to complain to a supervisory authority. Email us to exercise any of these.

Children

Sayal is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and we verify age from the date of birth you provide. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are hashed with Argon2. Sessions use short-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email.
