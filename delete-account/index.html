Delete your Sayal account

Applies to the Sayal app on iOS and Android

You can delete your Sayal account and all of its data yourself, from inside the app, in under a minute. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

From the app

  1. Open Sayal and go to Settings (the profile tab).
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.
  4. Tap Delete permanently. You will be signed out and your account is gone.

If you cannot access the app

Email isamalfuqaha@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Sayal account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 7 days, then confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, country and preferences.
  • All sign-in sessions.
  • Your simulated portfolio: cash balance, positions, orders, watchlist and portfolio history.

Everything above is removed from our live database immediately. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. Anonymous security logs that contain no account identifiers may be retained for up to 90 days.

What is not deleted

Nothing else is kept. Because Sayal never handles real money, there are no financial records we are required to retain.
