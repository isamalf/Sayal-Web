FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Sayal?

Sayal is a financial technology app built for Qatar and the GCC that makes investing easy. You can explore the US stock market, practise risk-free with a virtual portfolio, set personal financial goals, and link a third-party brokerage account to follow your investments in one simple place.

Is there a way to practise investing without risking money?

Yes. Practice Mode gives you a risk-free 100,000 QAR virtual portfolio so you can learn how the market works and build confidence before linking your brokerage account. No real money is involved in Practice Mode.

Can I connect my brokerage account?

Yes. Link a third-party brokerage account to view your portfolio and place self-directed trades through the app at your direction.

Can I use Sayal in Arabic?

Yes. Sayal offers a simple bilingual experience in Arabic and English, and you can view prices in QAR or USD.

How do I track my financial goals in Sayal?

Set personal wealth and savings goals in the Goals tab, then follow your progress over time with clear visual indicators inside the app.

How do I download the Sayal app?

Sayal is free to download and live on iOS and Android. Head to the download page to get started.
